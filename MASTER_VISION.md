## 3. The Supreme Vision (The Protocol)
As of January 2026, the Engine follows the **Supreme Vision Modification Plan**, designed to fundamentally alter the psychology of work and learning.

### I. The Neural Identity Gate
Standard registration is abolished. Users enter via a Socratic dialogue with **Sage** (the AI Mentor) to determine their love, talent, and problem-solving focus. This immediately establishes the mentor-protege relationship.

### II. The Sovereign Vault
Earnings are displayed as "Locked Gold" and "Unlocked Assets."
*   **Locked Gold**: Real currency that accumulates but requires "Genesis Points" (impact milestones) to unlock. This triggers "Loss Aversion" to motivate work.
*   **Unlocked Assets**: Intellectual property and code that the user owns until payment is released.

### III. The Skill Spark Feed (Genesis Engine)
Labor is rebranded as "Opportunity." The "Job Feed" is replaced by the **Genesis Feed**.
*   **Genesis Points**: High-impact problems from enterprise clients (e.g., "Optimize Solar Grid Data").
*   **Hidden Syllabus**: Every task embeds national academic standards (Algebra, Physics, Civics) without labels. Users learn by solving.
*   **Ripple Effect**: Every completed task displays its real-world impact (e.g., "Saves 400kWh/day").

### IV. Synaptic Squads
High-value solves require collaboration. Users form squads based on their Archetype:
*   **Visionary**: Sets the direction.
*   **Scribe**: Documents the process.
*   **Analyst**: Crunches the data.
*   **Sovereign**: The individual contributor.
Payouts are split by contribution weight (The Sovereign 2.0 Split).

## 4. Platform Design Architecture
The Engine is built on six immutable pillars:

### 1. Access & Onboarding
No forms. No age check. User opens browser, enters persistent world. Sage opens conversation: *"What did you finish last? Let’s build on it."*

### 2. Learning Engine (Just-in-Time Learning)
Curriculum emerges from live problems. When output fails—bridge buckles, code crashes—Sage triggers a micro-lesson: *"See the force vector? Forty-five seconds."* Use the hint (pay a small fee) or retry blind. Mastery is proven by reuse.

### 3. Revenue Distribution
Fee structure is variable and transparent. James Morris (Founder) sets weekly allocations displayed in a real-time governance ticker.
*   **Sovereign 2.0 Split**: 45% Solver, 15% Squad, 10% Ops, 10% Legal, 10% AI, 10% Growth Fund.

### 4. Collaboration Framework
Users join squads based on mission: "Clean Energy Squad", "Food Supply Chain". Cross-age, cross-border teams solve one bounty together.

### 5. Assessment Method
**Failure is data, not shame.** There are no grades. There is only "Definition of Done." A user's profile is a collection of successful "Solves" (verified digital assets).

### 6. Output & Transition
By age sixteen, the Vault is a resume: twenty assets, eight hundred dollars earned, client testimonials. Employers pull profiles via API. No transcript required.
